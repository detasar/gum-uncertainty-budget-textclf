## Related work (all links opened and titles/authors checked on 2026-10-17)

**Metrology framing.** The vocabulary is JCGM 200:2012 (VIM 3), especially 2.27 *definitional uncertainty* (https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.27.html), 2.21 repeatability and 2.25 reproducibility conditions; the evaluation procedure is JCGM 100:2008 (GUM; https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf), whose D.3.4 calls the same concept *intrinsic uncertainty* and whose 3.3.2 lists "incomplete definition of the measurand" (a) separately from "imperfect realization of the definition" (b) and "approximations and assumptions incorporated in the measurement method and procedure" (i). Petri & Mari, *Uncertainty Evaluation: Bridging Machine Learning and Measurement Science* (arXiv:2610.18066, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18066) argue for exactly this bridge; v1 and v2 are an empirical instance of it.

**Prompt / format / verbalizer sensitivity.** Sclar et al., *Quantifying Language Models' Sensitivity to Spurious Features in Prompt Design* (FormatSpread; arXiv:2310.11324); Mizrahi et al., *State of What Art? A Call for Multi-Prompt LLM Evaluation* (arXiv:2401.00595); Zhuo et al., *ProSA* (arXiv:2410.12405); Chatterjee et al., *POSIX: A Prompt Sensitivity Index* (arXiv:2410.02185); Weber, Bruni & Hupkes, *Mind the instructions* (arXiv:2310.13486); Polo et al., *Efficient multi-prompt evaluation of LLMs* (arXiv:2405.17202); Gonen et al., *Demystifying Prompts in Language Models via Perplexity Estimation* (arXiv:2212.04037); Pecher, Spiegel & Belanec, *Revisiting Prompt Sensitivity in LLMs for Text Classification: The Role of Prompt Underspecification* (arXiv:2602.04297); Zheng et al., *Large Language Models Are Not Robust Multiple Choice Selectors* (arXiv:2309.03882); Holtzman et al., *Surface Form Competition* (arXiv:2104.08315) and Zhao et al., *Calibrate Before Use* (arXiv:2102.09690), whose content-free calibration is the PMI scoring used for T3. Directly on label names: Sun, Xu & Shi, *Type-Safe Is Not Error-Free: Typed Decision Models Follow the Option Name, Not the Definition Bound to It* (arXiv:2609.26758). On annotation instruments: Reiter, Kern & Miasnikov, *Reliable but Design-Sensitive: Instrument Uncertainty in LLM Annotation* (arXiv:2609.35824). These works establish that wording matters for accuracy; v2's contribution is narrower: putting the wording effect into a variance-component budget next to sampling, with CIs, a validity-checked paraphrase population, and n-scaling, and testing whether "it does not shrink with data" licenses the word "definitional".

**Zero-shot classification via NLI.** Yin, Hay & Roth (arXiv:1909.00161); Laurer et al., *Building Efficient Universal Classifiers with Natural Language Inference* (arXiv:2312.17543; the ModernBERT zero-shot model used here is from this line of work).

**Conformal prediction.** Sadinle, Lei & Wasserman (LAC; arXiv:1609.00451); Romano, Sesia & Candès (APS; arXiv:2006.02544); Angelopoulos et al. (RAPS; arXiv:2009.14193); Angelopoulos & Bates, *A Gentle Introduction* (arXiv:2107.07511). For LLMs: Kumar et al., *Conformal Prediction with LLMs for Multi-Choice QA* (arXiv:2305.18404); Quach et al., *Conformal Language Modeling* (arXiv:2306.10193); Su et al., *API Is Enough* (arXiv:2403.01216); Ye et al., *Benchmarking LLMs via Uncertainty Quantification* (arXiv:2401.12794); Zhu, Yu & Li, *Conformalized LLMs under Configuration Shift* (arXiv:2608.01460) — closest to our cross-wording coverage check; Xin et al., *Conformal Prediction with Paraphrase-Aware Scoring* (arXiv:2610.04239); Stutz et al., *Conformal prediction under ambiguous ground truth* (arXiv:2307.09302).

**Variance in benchmarks, run-to-run variance, determinism.** Bouthillier et al., *Accounting for Variance in ML Benchmarks* (arXiv:2103.03098); Hassanzadeh & Subramanian, *Same Query, Different Answer* (arXiv:2610.13823); Qin, Du & Zhang, *Vosti: deterministic LLM inference* (arXiv:2609.38981).

**Contamination.** Shi et al., *Detecting Pretraining Data from LLMs* (Min-K% Prob; arXiv:2310.16789); Sainz et al., *NLP Evaluation in trouble* (arXiv:2310.18018); Li et al., *LatestEval* (arXiv:2312.12343; the RealTimeData BBC corpus used for BBC-2025 comes from this project); Sharma, Duncan & Caron, *TypedBench* (arXiv:2610.11392).

**Label variation.** Plank, *The "Problem" of Human Label Variation* (arXiv:2211.02570).

**Datasets.** AG News and DBpedia-14: Zhang, Zhao & LeCun (arXiv:1509.01626); SST-2: Socher et al. 2013 (EMNLP, https://aclanthology.org/D13-1170/); MASSIVE: FitzGerald et al. (arXiv:2204.08582); BBC 2024-10…2025-06: Hugging Face dataset `RealTimeData/bbc_news_alltime`.
